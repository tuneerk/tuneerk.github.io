01 / INGEST—About

Instrumentation → deep learning → production ML.

I started in instrumentation and electronics at NIT Jalandhar, then moved into data science and deep learning — first on computer vision for agriculture, then at Samsung Research building on-device AI: emotion recognition, image effects, and sensor models that run entirely on a phone, not a server.

Now I'm an AI Engineer at Google, building end-to-end ML systems on GCP — forecasting frameworks powered by LLMs, retrieval-augmented chat, the kind of work that has to hold up in production, not just in a notebook. Photography stays in the loop too: I ran a university photography club, and I still shoot — mostly travel and street work — for the same reason I like ML: deciding what to keep in frame and what to leave out.
