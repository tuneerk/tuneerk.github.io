Profile

My journey is fueled by a dual passion: the intricate logic of AI and the evocative art of photography. It started with an engineering background at NIT Jalandhar and early explorations in data science — the same stretch of time I spent leading the university's photography club, learning to see before I learned to build.

"The logic of AI and the art of photography — different tools, same instinct: notice the pattern, then decide what's worth keeping."

At Samsung Research, I spent over two years sharpening that instinct inside Deep Learning and On-Device AI: shrinking models to fit on a phone, teaching cameras to read emotion, and shipping features that ran on millions of Galaxy devices. Two of those years turned into published research — first at ISCAS on emotion recognition, then at ISEC on human activity monitoring.

Now, as an AI Engineer at Google, I build end-to-end ML solutions with GCP — trend-forecasting frameworks, retrieval-augmented chatbots, the unglamorous plumbing that makes a model useful. The throughline hasn't changed: whether I'm training a network or framing a shot, the work is the same discipline of paying close attention, then deciding what's worth keeping.
